Before you begin

This informal questionnaire has 35 questions in five parts. Choose the alternative that best describes you. If neither fits exactly, choose the one closer to your preference.

Important: Learning preferences are not fixed traits, and this questionnaire is not a validated measure of learning ability. Research does not establish that matching teaching to a reported “learning style” improves outcomes. Use your results to reflect on strategies to try, not to limit what or how you learn.
